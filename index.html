Eco Score Evaluation Methodology

How We Measure Eco Sustainability

Every destination is scored out of 100 based on three transparent environmental and cultural preservation parameters.

1. Nature Protection (40%)

Evaluating biodiversity preservation, forest canopy safety, water conservation, and zero-plastic policies in national parks.

2. Waste Management (30%)

Monitoring composting systems, segregation of organic waste, and non-intrusive eco-friendly visitor trails.

3. Local Business (30%)

Ensuring direct financial flow to local tribal guides, homestays, indigenous artisans, and traditional eateries.
